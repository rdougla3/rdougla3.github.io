# About

I am a full-stack software engineer in Dallas, Texas, focused on enterprise DevSecOps products, reliability, and secure delivery platforms. I build platform software that turns complex delivery infrastructure into repeatable self-service workflows, including environment provisioning, CI/CD pipeline configuration, security tool integration, artifact management, and operational automation.

I earned a B.S. in Computer Science from Arizona State University in December 2023 and am pursuing an M.C.S. in Cybersecurity at Arizona State University, with expected graduation in December 2027.

Outside of work and school, I race drones, am an active member and volunteer at the Dallas Makerspace, and spend time climbing. I also spend a lot of my free time painting miniatures and working on other random maker projects.
